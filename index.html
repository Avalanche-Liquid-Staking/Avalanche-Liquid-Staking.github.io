What is Avalanche Liquid Staking?

BENQI is the Avalanche DeFi protocol for staking AVAX into sAVAX, a transferable receipt token that represents a staked position. The BENQI docs describe staking from C-Chain and using sAVAX across DeFi.

How Avalanche Liquid Staking works

  1. Open the appGo to app.benqi.fi/savax and confirm the domain before connecting a wallet.
  2. Connect on C-ChainUse an Avalanche C-Chain wallet with AVAX available for staking and network gas.
  3. Stake AVAXEnter the AVAX amount, review the transaction, and receive sAVAX in the same wallet.
  4. Manage exitUse the unstake and claim flow when needed; BENQI documents a 15-day cooldown and 2-day redemption window.

Before you stake

Liquid receipt

sAVAX represents staked AVAX and accrues rewards through its exchange rate instead of requiring a locked wallet balance.

C-Chain flow

BENQI lets users stake from Avalanche C-Chain while the protocol handles the staking path behind the app.

Check before signing

Use the official app, review wallet prompts, and read the BENQI risk notes before depositing funds.

Avalanche Liquid Staking FAQ

What is avalanche liquid staking?

It is staking AVAX through a liquid-staking protocol such as BENQI, where users receive sAVAX as a transferable representation of their staked position.

How do I start avalanche liquid staking in the official app?

Open app.benqi.fi/savax, connect an Avalanche C-Chain wallet, enter an AVAX amount, review the transaction, and stake.

Does avalanche liquid staking lock my AVAX?

sAVAX remains transferable, but using BENQI's unstake route has a documented cooldown before AVAX can be claimed.

What token do I receive from avalanche liquid staking?

BENQI issues sAVAX, which represents the staked AVAX position and accrued Avalanche staking rewards.

What risks apply to avalanche liquid staking?

Smart-contract risk, validator reward risk, liquidity conditions, and secondary-market pricing can all matter; review official docs and verify the app domain before signing.
